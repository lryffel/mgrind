# Make fractions in linear equations much easier

## Description

Make the fractions in linear equations much easier.

## Acceptance Criteria

- At complexity ≤ 6, no fractions appear in either the equation coefficients or the solution (all integers)
- Fraction denominators are limited to 2 (only halves), both in coefficients and in the solution
- Fraction numerators are limited to ±1 (only `1/2` and `-1/2`)
- At complexity 7–8, fraction coefficients do not appear; only the solution may be `±1/2`
- At complexity 9–10, fractions may also appear as coefficients, still limited to `±1/2`
- Fraction probability for the solution is reduced from 30 % to 20 %
- Fraction probability for coefficients (at complexity 9–10) is reduced from 30 % to 15 %
